# Art Factory style contract

Generic original fantasy RPG pixel-art assets, not a specific game's artwork. Elevated three-quarter top-down view, consistent warm top-left lighting, intentionally clustered square pixels, coherent colors and restrained outlines. Prioritize beautiful silhouettes, clarity and cross-batch cohesion. Four related objects per generated sheet, arranged in the exact quadrants shown in the queue. Large empty gutter; do not overlap/crop objects. Prefer flat extraction-friendly background, real transparent source if supported. Never assume the generator can achieve exact canvas sizes—EXTRACTOR owns pixel dimensions, background removal, anchors and format. Final placeables use RGBA PNG and anchor coordinates from `art/queue.json`. Batches 15–16 are 16x16 terrain tiles; EXTRACTOR must construct and review genuinely repeatable textures, not merely crop four source pictures. No copyrighted game art imitation. Approved artwork can later become a reference sheet; placeholders are NOT style references.
